Data Governance

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 | Last Updated: September 2026

At SOW — Spare On Wheel (“SOW”, “we”, “our”, or “us”), protecting your personal privacy and the integrity of your automotive data is fundamental to our engineering culture. This Privacy Policy details how information is collected, processed, and safeguarded across the SOW website, customer mobile application, Digital Vehicle Passport, and connected partner ecosystem.

1. The SOW Data Philosophy

Unlike ad-supported platforms, our business model is built on providing exceptional automotive technology and connected ecosystem services. We do not sell, rent, or trade your vehicle telemetry, location logs, or personal identity to third-party data brokers or advertising networks.

2. Information We Collect

A. Vehicle Identification & Specifications

When you register a vehicle on SOW, we collect vehicle identification numbers (VIN), license registration marks, make, model, variant, year of manufacture, and engine specifications to accurately populate your Digital Vehicle Passport and ensure spare parts compatibility.

B. Vehicle Health & Maintenance Records

We collect and generate telemetry health assessments, odometer readings, service receipts, inspection summaries, and user-input symptoms to track component longevity and provide predictive maintenance alerts.

C. Emergency SOS & Location Data

Precise real-time GPS location is accessed only when you activate an Emergency SOS request or track a 45-minute parts delivery. Location access is strictly ephemeral and used solely to dispatch and coordinate nearby responders.

D. Document Vault

Registration certificates (RC), pollution under control certificates (PUC), insurance policies, and service invoices uploaded to the Document Vault are encrypted in transit and at rest using modern encryption standards.

3. How Your Information is Utilized

  • Generating and maintaining your immutable Digital Vehicle Passport.
  • Powering SOW AI diagnostic guidance and part identification algorithms.
  • Matching compatible parts on the SOW Marketplace with zero fitment guesswork.
  • Dispatching verified technicians and towing responders during roadside emergencies.
  • Reminding vehicle owners of upcoming statutory renewals and scheduled maintenance intervals.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We share necessary vehicle data only with authorized parties upon your explicit instruction:

  • Service Workshops & Mobile Mechanics: When you confirm a booking or purchase a part requiring bay installation.
  • Emergency Responders: Your location, vehicle model, and reported issue are shared strictly during active SOS sessions.
  • Parts Suppliers: Anonymous vehicle fitment requirements are communicated to fulfill orders.

5. Your Rights & Data Portability

You maintain full ownership of your vehicle history. You have the right to inspect, export, or permanently delete your account and associated Document Vault files at any time by contacting our privacy officer.
