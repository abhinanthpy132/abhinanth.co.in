Security

No compromises.

Security starts with Apple silicon and extends to the macOS architecture. This deep integration of hardware and software, along with automatic updates and built-in antivirus protection, helps keep MacBook Pro stable and secure for the long term. At the heart of MacBook Pro is the Secure Enclave, a dedicated security subsystem that’s separate from the main processor. It keeps your most sensitive data, like encryption keys and biometric information, fully isolated from the rest of the system — the same protection that powers Touch ID and Find My.

    Recycled Material

    Our approach.

    Using recycled materials, like 100% recycled cobalt in the battery, reduces the need to mine new material, which avoids the carbon emissions and environmental impacts of mining.

     Learn how to trade in or recycle devices

  • Manufactured with 50% renewable electricity.

    Renewable Electricity

    Our approach.

    Using electricity from renewable sources like wind and solar — instead of fossil fuels — across our global supply chain significantly reduces carbon emissions from manufacturing Apple products.

    Learn more in our product environmental reports:

     14″ MacBook Pro M6 (PDF), opens in new tab 14″ and 16″ MacBook Pro M5 Pro and M5 Max (PDF), opens in new tab

    How you can help.

    You can extend the lifespan of your batteries and Apple products by using Optimised Battery Charging. It works by learning your charging habits and delaying the final charge — from 80% to 100% — until you are likely to need it.
